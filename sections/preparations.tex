\section{Preparation and Prerequisites}\label{sec:prep_pre}

What do you need to complete the project?
We will use , 1-3 contemporary news events

We will need access to Large Language Models that are accessible to the public. To that end, we have decided to test ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and DeepSeek.
In addition, we will access news publications with rigorous fact-checking standards [should replace with specific institutions once we decide], particularly articles that corroborate the veracity of our particular news events.

Our data will be in the form of semantically-coded responses from the LLMs to the prompts from our experimental user profiles, which will then be compared to the baseline truth statement gathered from the news publications
and rated based on their veracity, specificity, and markers of censorship.
