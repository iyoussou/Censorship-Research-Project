\section{Preparation and Prerequisites}\label{sec:prep_pre}

To complete this project, we will select 3 contemporary news events. 

We will use LLMs that are accessible to and commonly used by the public. To that end, we have decided to test ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and DeepSeek.
In addition, we will access news publications with rigorous fact-checking standards (will be replaced by specific institutions once we decide), particularly articles that corroborate the veracity of our particular news events.

Our data will be in the form of semantically-coded responses from the LLMs to the prompts from our experimental user profiles, which will then be compared to the baseline truth statement gathered from the news publications and rated based on their veracity, specificity, and markers of censorship.
